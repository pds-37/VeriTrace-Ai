### Intended Use
* **Purpose**: Colorimetric and image-processing methodology benchmark only.
* **Non-Diagnostic Constraint**: This dataset is used strictly for evaluating image-processing algorithms, Region of Interest (ROI) cropping, and colorimetric calibration methodology.
* **No Substance Classification**: None of these images represent, claim, or should be labeled as confirmed drug-positive or drug-negative samples.
* **Isolation**: This benchmark dataset is strictly separated from field application capture data and future project demonstrations.
